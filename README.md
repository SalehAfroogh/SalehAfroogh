## Hi, I'm Saleh 👋

**AI Research Staff at The University of Texas at Austin** · Research Scientist at [SafeKidAI](https://safekidai.org/)

I work on **responsible AI/LLMs, reasoning, and interpretable AI** — building safety benchmarks, evaluation pipelines, and end-to-end ML tooling (PyTorch, scikit-learn) that probe LLMs for hallucination, bias, sycophancy, and harmful outputs. I led [The Responsible AI Initiative](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative), manage the [Responsible AI portfolio](https://ethicalai.utexas.edu/portfolio-program/) in UT Austin's NSF NRT program, and direct a cross-national explainable AI (XAI) project with 49 scholars from IBM, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, MIT, Stanford, and Harvard, among others.

My work has appeared in *Nature Human Behaviour*, *Scientific Reports*, AAAI/ACM AIES, and ICLR workshops.

### 🛠️ Selected projects

| Project | What it does | Venue |
|---|---|---|
| [LLM Ethics Benchmark](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/LLM_Ethics_Benchmark) | Three-dimensional benchmark scoring how closely LLMs track human moral judgments across five moral foundations | *Scientific Reports* 2025 |
| [DVF Sycophancy Eval](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/dvf-sycophancy-eval) | Age-calibrated probes measuring LLM sycophancy toward young users; introduces the Developmental Vulnerability Function | AAAI/ACM AIES 2026 |
| [GPT Parental Controls Benchmark](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/GPT-parental-controls-benchmark) | Audits whether ChatGPT parental controls actually notify parents across seven risk areas | AAAI/ACM AIES 2026 |
| [Safe-Child-LLM](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/Safe_Child_LLM_Evaluation) | 200 adversarial prompts for children and teens, scored on a 0–5 refusal scale across eight LLMs | arXiv 2025 |
| [SafeGesture](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/SafeGesture) | 4,800-item benchmark testing whether vision-language models turn a recognized gesture into the right safety action | Benchmark |
| [ChildSafe SDK](https://github.com/The-Responsible-AI-Initiative/ChildSafe) | Simulates children at different developmental stages to audit chatbots over multi-turn conversations | Open-source SDK |
| [Proof Systems & LLM Reasoning](https://huggingface.co/SalehAfroogh) | Llama-3.1-8B fine-tuned on 110k logic problems in Suppes–Lemmon, Fitch, and Gentzen LK proof styles | Ongoing |
